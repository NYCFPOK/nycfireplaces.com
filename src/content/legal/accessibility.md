We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who rely on assistive technology. We build and test the site with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our goal: readable text and contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus, descriptive labels on forms and images, and respect for reduced-motion settings.

## If something does not work for you

Please tell us. Call [718-326-4328](tel:+17183264328) or [send us a message](/project-help/) describing the page and the problem, and we will help you get the information or service you need and work on a fix.

## In person

If you plan to visit the showroom and have accessibility questions, call ahead and we will be glad to help.
