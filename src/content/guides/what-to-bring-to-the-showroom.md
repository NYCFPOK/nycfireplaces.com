You can walk in with nothing but an idea. If you want to get more out of the visit, these help.

## For any project

- A few phone photos of the room or yard, taken from different angles.
- Approximate dimensions of the wall or area.
- Pictures of fireplaces or kitchens you like, even if you do not know what they are.
- Plans or drawings, if a renovation or build is under way.

## For an existing fireplace

- A straight-on photo of the fireplace, and one looking into the firebox.
- The width and height of the opening at the front, the width at the back, and the depth.
- Whether there is gas in the house.

## For an outdoor kitchen

- A photo of where it will go, showing the house wall if it is nearby.
- Where gas, water and power are, if you know.
- How many people you usually cook for.

## Before you come

If there is a specific model you want to see, call ahead. Not everything we sell is on the floor, and we would rather tell you before you make the trip.
