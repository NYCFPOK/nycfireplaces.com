# Editing guide for staff

Go to `/admin/` on the website and sign in with GitHub. Changes are saved to the site's repository and the site rebuilds. Nothing here needs a developer.

> Set-up needed once: each editor needs a GitHub account with write access to the repository. See "Content editor" in LAUNCH-AND-OPERATIONS.md.

## Common jobs

**Change hours, add a holiday closure**
Business details → Maspeth showroom → Regular hours, or Holiday and special hours. Add the date as `2026-12-25`, leave Opens and Closes empty for closed, and add a note such as "Christmas Day". Hours update on every page, in the footer, and in the information Google reads.

**Change the phone number or address**
Business details. Every page, button and footer updates together.

**Open the Greenvale showroom**
Business details → Other locations → set Status to `open` and rewrite the note. Ask the developer to add its hours block when it opens.

**Change the featured shortlist**
Featured shortlist. Each entry has an Order, a "Show on homepage" switch, and "On display in showroom?". Keep six on the homepage and three per starting path. Do not call anything a best seller unless sales figures support it.

**Add a project**
Projects → New. Add a title, year, category and photos. Only add a location or a designer credit when the customer or designer has agreed. Fill in the goal, what was selected, what our team did and the lesson only with details you know are correct. Leave the rest empty.

**Add a customer review**
Reviews. Paste the review exactly as written, with the name as it appears publicly and a link to the original. Tick "Approved to publish". Never write or reword a review.

**Add or change an FAQ**
FAQs. "Where it shows" controls which pages list it.

**Run a promotion**
Promotions. An end date is required.

**Change button wording**
Business details → Button wording.

## Rules that protect the business

- No prices unless the owner has approved the wording. Never enter 0 as a price.
- No "free", "guaranteed", "same day" or "best" claims without sign-off.
- Use only real photos of your own work and showroom.
- If you are not sure a fact is true for every customer, leave it out or add "depending on the project".
